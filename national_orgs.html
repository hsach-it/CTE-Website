NATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS

Our National Organizations

At Horizon, we participate in a multitude of national organizations in which we have succeeded at the state and national levels. Our students compete alongside peers from across the country, building leadership, technical skills, and professional networks that last a lifetime.

Business & IT

BPA – Business Professionals of America

In our first year participating, we succeeded at both regional and state levels — and our students are currently competing at the national level. BPA prepares students for careers in business, finance, management, and IT through competitive events and leadership development.

Health Science

HOSA – Health Occupations Students of America

HOSA empowers students pursuing careers in healthcare and biomedical sciences. Our members compete in health science events, participate in community service, and develop the professional skills needed for success in the medical field.

Trade & Technical

SkillsUSA

SkillsUSA is a national organization serving students in technical, skilled, and service occupations. Our students compete in areas ranging from engineering and manufacturing to cybersecurity and health, demonstrating real-world expertise on a national stage.
